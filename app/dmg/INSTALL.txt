NOTETAKER: local meeting notes for your Mac
==========================================

Records any call (Teams, Zoom, Slack, Meet in a browser), transcribes it ON YOUR MAC
(Latvian, English and Russian), and writes an English summary with decisions and action items.

Needs: an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 15 or later, and about 12 GB of free disk space
for the speech models, which download automatically the first time.

INSTALL
1. Drag Notetaker onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Applications, RIGHT-CLICK Notetaker › Open › Open.
   (It isn't from the App Store, so a normal double-click is blocked the first time.
    If you don't see an Open button, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down,
    and click "Open Anyway".)
3. A waveform icon appears in the menu bar. If your menu bar is full, it may be hidden behind the
   notch; ⌘-drag a few icons off the bar to make room.

FIRST RECORDING
- Click the icon › Start Recording. Allow "Microphone" and "Screen & System Audio Recording".
  (It only records audio, never your screen.) Then quit Notetaker and open it again.
- When a call starts, a card pops up at the top right: "Teams call detected — Start notes?".
  About a minute after the call ends, notes are made and open in your browser.
- The first meeting takes a few extra minutes while the speech model (~3 GB) downloads.

SUMMARIES
- "Summarize with Claude" uses your Claude login. Either sign in to the `claude` command once
  (run `claude` in Terminal, then type /login), or install the Notetaker plugin in the Claude
  desktop app and let it summarize queued meetings for you.
- "Summarize Locally (private)" keeps everything on your Mac (downloads an ~8 GB model once).

Notes are saved in ~/Notes/meetings. Source and plugin: https://github.com/fridapirz/local-notetaker
